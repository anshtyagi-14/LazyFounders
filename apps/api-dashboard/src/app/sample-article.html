Mother’s Day is not just about flowers and cards, it is about expressing gratitude and love through thoughtful gestures. Whether your mum prefers luxury, wellness, art, or practicality, this curated list brings together some of the most meaningful and premium gifts from homegrown Indian brands. Celebrate her with something truly special this year.

Inside the move

Forest Essentials, one of India's most beloved luxury Ayurvedic brands, crafts products rooted in ancient Ayurvedic texts using pure, natural ingredients. A timeless choice for gifting.

Their curated gift sets, featuring face serums, body butters, and facial oils, make for an indulgent and deeply nourishing treat for moms. Beautifully packaged and richly formulated, these products transform everyday skincare into a luxurious ritual, making them one of India's most popular Mother's Day gifts.

KorinMi is India's first professional Korean skin clinic based in Gurugram, bringing advanced Korean skincare technology with personalised treatments thoughtfully tailored for Indian skin.

The SA Ampoule Toner is a thoughtful Mother's Day gift that invites moms to slow down and prioritise themselves. Infused with antioxidants and peptides, it hydrates, firms, and restores skin's natural balance, turning a daily routine into a meaningful self-care ritual with radiant results.

UltraCare PRO is an Indian wellness brand offering a range of clinically backed devices designed for everyday pain relief, relaxation, and recovery, making quality physiotherapy accessible from the comfort of home.

Your mother is the first to wake up and the last to rest, always giving, always doing, always there. This Mother's Day, give her comfort she can truly feel. The UNIQ Head and Scalp Massager soothes daily stress, the UNIQ Neck and Shoulder Massager releases deep-seated tension, the wireless TENS 2.0 targets everyday aches, and the UltraCure Electric Hot Water Bag delivers warm, lasting relief, because she deserves care that goes beyond just one day.

Founded in 1999 by Gaurav Bhagat, Consortium Gifts is Asia's leading corporate gifting firm, blending innovation, AI-led manufacturing, and global partnerships to redefine brand experiences through quality and tech-driven solutions.

This Mother's Day, Consortium Gifts curates a power-packed lineup for the modern, multitasking mom, from sleek laptop sleeves and smart hydration tumblers to portable kettles and compact fitness tools. Each product is designed to elevate efficiency while adding comfort, convenience, and a touch of everyday luxury to her routine.

Frissly is an emerging leader in India's clean and organic food space, crafting products using certified organic, 100% authentic ingredients sourced from trusted farmers across the Hyderabad region.

Frissly offers a thoughtfully curated range of clean-label foods, from organic snacks, batters, and sweets to breakfast options and everyday staples. Made fresh in batches and free from preservatives, refined sugars, and chemicals, these products combine nutrition with taste, making them a practical, guilt-free gift for health-conscious moms.

Healthy Master is a homegrown healthy snacking brand offering 250+ clean-label products, from millet snacks and energy bars to roasted nuts, makhana, and dry fruits, guided by the philosophy of making it easier to switch to healthier choices.

The Daily Power Booster, a doctor-recommended blend of 23+ exotic nuts, seeds, and antioxidant-rich berries, is perfect for energetic, busy moms. Pair it with their Beetroot Chips, Millet Noodles, or Crispy Fox Nuts for a wholesome, delicious gifting hamper she'll genuinely love.

The numbers behind the story

Abhi Eggs is a premium nutrition-focused egg brand headquartered in Hyderabad, known for India's first DOSE Egg, antibiotic-free and fortified with Vitamin D3, Omega 3, DHA, and Vitamin B12 to support preventive nutrition and healthy living.

For the mom who nourishes everyone else, gift her something that nourishes her back. Abhi Eggs' Vitamin D3-enriched eggs support bone strength, immunity, and sustained energy, and two eggs a day is all it takes. Fresh, clean, and packed with essential nutrients, it's a practical yet deeply thoughtful gift for mothers who prioritise their health and wellbeing.

Shrupaneel is a sustainable, biodiversity-friendly honey brand sourcing seasonal mono-floral honey from verified beekeepers and tribal communities in Madhya Pradesh. Every jar is raw, unprocessed, and traceable.

Their Mono-Floral Raw Honey Gift Pack includes three distinct honeys: Wild Tulsi for immunity, Ajwain for digestive health, and Jamun for low-GI wellness. Each 75g jar is free from added sugar, artificial colours, and preservatives. Beautifully boxed and handcrafted, it's a wholesome gift that tells a story of nature and care.

Pickle Shickle is a family-led artisanal brand preserving legacy recipes through handcrafted pickles, Burmese chilli oils, and unique condiments, spanning vegetarian, non-vegetarian, and regional specialities made with love and tradition.

What better Mother's Day gift than one steeped in five generations of love? Shrimply Burm-Ease, their Burmese Crispy Onion & Garlic Chilli Oil with shrimp, is an original recipe passed down from the founder's great-great-grandmother, celebrating the most timeless bond of all: mother and daughter. Smoky, crispy, and deeply flavourful, it's a jar full of heritage.

Yoga Bars is one of India's most trusted nutrition brands, offering clean, wholesome snacks and superfoods, including protein bars, muesli, granola, and nut butters with no artificial additives.

Their gift hampers, packed with protein bars, trail mixes, and healthy breakfast options, make for a practical yet thoughtful gift for moms who value wellness. Widely available across India, Yoga Bars are a reliable choice for gifting nutrition-conscious mothers who deserve to snack guilt-free.

Hexafun is a playful Indian lifestyle accessories brand transforming everyday essentials into vibrant, quirky expressions of individuality. Founded in 2021, all products are crafted from 100% sustainable materials.

The Jammin'! Tin Gift Box, a set of 12 unisex handkerchiefs in 100% organic cotton, is a delightfully quirky and practical gift for moms. Colourful, sustainable, and packaged with personality, it's the kind of thoughtful gift that brings a smile while being genuinely useful every single day.

Indimums is disrupting Indian baby care by replacing complex chemical-laden formulations with time-tested botanicals. Known for launching the world's first Reetha-based, SLS-free baby range, the brand puts ingredient transparency first.

Their five-product baby care hamper includes a Reetha & Shikakai Baby Shampoo, 17-herb Hair Oil, soap-free Body Wash, pure plant-based Body Butter, and a toxin-free Handwash. Every ingredient is one a parent can read and trust, making it a genuinely meaningful gift for new and seasoned moms alike.

What this changes

Beyond Bound is a women-first activewear brand built on versatility, performance, and inclusive fits. Crafted with premium fabrics and HD logos, each piece is designed to move with you and instil confidence.

This Mother's Day, gift her the FlowTwist Pants with a flattering flare and a twisted waistband, the adjustable Flex Tie Vest, or the motivational Flex It Babe Socks. Together, they make a curated activewear set for the mom who loves staying active and moving through life on her own terms.

Bluetyga is a performance-driven lifestyle brand redefining outdoor essentials by combining fashion with functionality, crafting smart, protective apparel designed for India's extreme weather conditions and evolving consumer needs.

This Mother's Day, gift the mom who is always on the go India's first Sunscreen Jacket Pro. Powered by advanced Sunguard technology, it offers certified UPF protection with 99.9% UVA & UVB defence. Breathable, lightweight, and foldable into a compact pouch, it's a stylish yet deeply practical gift that shields her every time she steps out.

Phool.co is a beloved Indian social enterprise that upcycles temple flowers into luxury incense, natural colours, and wellness products. A brand with a beautiful story and a meaningful mission.

Their incense sticks, dhoop cones, and floral wellness sets make for a deeply soulful Mother's Day gift. Naturally fragrant and free from toxic charcoal or chemicals, gifting Phool to your mom means gifting her moments of calm, spirituality, and the warm comfort of home, all wrapped in an eco-conscious ethos.

A&Co. is a luxury craftsmanship house creating writing instruments, timepieces, and bespoke products rooted in emotion, memory, and precision, each piece designed to become a personal legacy.

The Eminence Fountain Pen is a deeply meaningful Mother's Day gift. Handcrafted with premium materials and a smooth stainless steel German Jowo #6 nib, it transforms everyday writing into a reflective ritual. With elegant finishes and personalisation options, it's not just a pen, it's a story she can write and carry forward.

Founded by Ms. Natasha Tuli, Soulflower is India’s first beauty brand to cultivate its own ingredients on farms in Rajasthan. With a focus on #CleanBeauty and #FarmToFace, Soulflower ensures each product is natural, ethical, and cruelty-free.

This Mother’s Day, the Kumkumadi Face Wash & Oil Combo is a perfect gift to brighten and revitalise tired skin. Infused with saffron, sandalwood, and wild turmeric, it offers a luxurious, glow-enhancing experience.

This Mother’s Day, celebrate the superwoman in your life with a gift that truly resonates with her personality, be it a meaningful piece of décor, a scientific skincare solution, or a luxurious fashion accessory. With these curated selections from standout Indian brands, you’re sure to find something she’ll treasure forever.
